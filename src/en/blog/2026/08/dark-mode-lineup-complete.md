The lineup for [Fronteers Dark Mode](https://fronteersconf.org/) is complete! On **Friday 2 October 2026**, we welcome you to Cinema De Witt in Dordrecht for a cozy, single-track evening of front-end talks — from 3PM until well after midnight.

## Speakers

These five speakers (and one MC) will take the stage:

- **Harry Roberts** — _Build for the web, build on the web, build with the web_
- **Cyd Stumpel** — _You might not need JS_
- **Barry Pollard** — _Declarative Partial Updates: Rethinking How the Web Streams HTML_
- **Anjana Vakil** — _Making Waves with the Web: Audio Synthesis and Data Sonification with the WebAudio API_
- **Heydon Pickering** — _Making HTML Make Music_
- **Jake Archibald** — Master of Ceremonies

Expect inspiring technical talks on HTML, CSS and JavaScript — plus dinner, drinks and a nerdy late-night finale with fellow front-end developers.

## Tickets

There are only ~100 seats, so don't wait too long. A regular ticket costs **€99** (including a two-course dinner and VAT). **Fronteers members pay only €49.** [Get your ticket at fronteersconf.org](https://fronteersconf.org/tickets/).
