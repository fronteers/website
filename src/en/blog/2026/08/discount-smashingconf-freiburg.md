Members of Fronteers, we have a discount for you! [SmashingConf Freiburg](https://smashingconf.com/freiburg-2026/) returns to the city where Smashing Magazine began, from **7 to 10 September 2026**. Two conference days with twelve speakers, plus optional workshop days — all in the historic Kaufhaus in the heart of Freiburg.

Topics include design systems, AI, accessibility, CSS, animation and cutting-edge front-end techniques. Speakers include Jake Archibald, Hidde de Vries, Marie van Driessche, Vitaly Friedman and more.

**Fronteers members get a 20% discount on tickets.** Mail the board at [bestuur@fronteers.nl](mailto:bestuur@fronteers.nl) to request the discount code and instructions.

With only 300 seats, tickets tend to sell out — so don't wait too long if you're planning to go!
