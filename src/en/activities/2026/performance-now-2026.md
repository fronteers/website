performance.now() is a single track conference covering today's most important web performance insights. Our speakers offer practical, actionable take-aways about JavaScript and CSS performance, performance of popular frameworks, performance culture, performance budgets, perceived performance, performance monitoring, and the post-Core-Web-Vital world.

**Dates:** November 19th and 20th, 2026  
**Location:** Zuiderkerk, Amsterdam  
**Speakers:** Harry Roberts, Stéphanie Walter, Tammy Everts, Tim Kadlec and Pat Meenan, with Robin Marx as MC
