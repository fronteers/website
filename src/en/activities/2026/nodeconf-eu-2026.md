If JavaScript and Node.js are your thing, take a look at [NodeConf.eu](https://nodeconf.eu/) — a community-driven event in beautiful Bologna, Italy, on **29 and 30 September 2026**.

Expect two days of talks on Node.js applications, runtimes, tooling, observability, architecture and production systems — with enough breathing room between sessions for the hallway track. On the evening of 29 September, the whole conference sits down together for a proper Bolognese evening of food, wine and conversation. It's included in your ticket.

Many of us still have fond memories of travelling to Bologna years ago for From the Front. It's a wonderful city: amazing food, beautiful streets and a fantastic atmosphere.

**Dates:** 29–30 September 2026  
**Location:** Hotel Savoia Regency, Bologna, Italy  
**Tickets:** €437.98 incl. VAT (regular price)
