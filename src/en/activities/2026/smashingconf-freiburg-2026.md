SmashingConf Freiburg 2026 returns to the city where Smashing Magazine began. A friendly, inclusive conference for front-end developers and designers, with two single-track conference days and optional workshop days before and after. Topics include design systems, AI, accessibility, CSS, animation and cutting-edge front-end techniques, with speakers such as Jake Archibald, Hidde de Vries, Marie van Driessche and Vitaly Friedman.

**Dates:** September 7–10, 2026  
**Location:** Historisches Kaufhaus, Freiburg, Germany

**Fronteers members receive a 20% discount. Contact the board for the required information!**
